# PHARMA UROLOGY RETAIL PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS

## Data Analyst Project Portfolio

Welcome to my **Data Analyst Project Portfolio**!  
In this repository, you'll find a detailed analysis of **retail sales performance in the Urology product category** across a nationwide pharmacy network.

This project aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of **sales performance, inventory allocation, lost sales, and assortment compliance**.  
The goal is to generate **data-driven insights and operational recommendations** to improve product availability and retail efficiency.

---

## About This Project

The primary goal of this project is to analyze the **performance of Urology products in a retail pharmacy system**, covering the following key areas:

• **Sales Performance:**  
Analyzing total revenue and sales volume across **899 pharmacy stores nationwide** to understand which regions and stores contribute the most to overall performance.

• **Product Performance:**  
Identifying the **top-performing SKUs** in terms of both revenue and quantity sold, and understanding differences between high-volume and high-value products.

• **Inventory Analysis:**  
Evaluating current inventory levels and identifying stores with **slow-moving inventory or stock imbalances**.

• **Lost Sales Analysis:**  
Investigating situations where customer demand could not be fulfilled due to stock shortages, assortment issues, or operational factors.

• **Assortment Compliance:**  
Assessing whether stores maintain the required assortment of products and identifying cases where missing SKUs may lead to lost sales opportunities.

## Key Insights

Through this project, several important business insights were identified:

• **Regional Sales Concentration**  
Ho Chi Minh City contributes nearly **50% of total revenue** and around **45% of total sales volume**, making it the most critical market for this category.

• **Manufacturer Dependency**  
Approximately **60% of total revenue** comes from two major manufacturers, indicating high supplier concentration.

• **High-Value Products**  
Some SKUs generate **high revenue despite relatively low sales volume**, suggesting strong potential for premium or high-margin products.

• **Inventory Imbalance**  
Although total inventory levels are high, around **28% of stores still experience slow-moving inventory**, indicating inefficient stock allocation.

• **Lost Sales Causes**  
Lost sales mainly occur due to:
- Stock shortages
- Missing assortment items
- Operational or system-related issues

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, several operational improvements are recommended:

• **Improve Supply Planning for Key Regions**  
Prioritize replenishment for high-demand SKUs in high-revenue regions.

• **Optimize Inventory Allocation**  
Implement store-to-store stock relocation to reduce slow-moving inventory.

• **Strengthen Assortment Management**  
Ensure stores maintain required SKUs to avoid missed sales opportunities.

• **Focus on High-Value Products**  
Prioritize forecasting and shelf availability for products that generate high revenue despite lower volume.

• **Investigate Operational Lost Orders**  
Analyze cases where stock existed but sales were still lost to identify operational inefficiencies.
